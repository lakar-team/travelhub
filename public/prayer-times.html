Prayer times in Tohoku, all year

See how Fajr to Isha shift through the seasons, and when Ramadan 2027 falls, in six Tohoku cities.

Through the year

Move over the chart to pick a day. The shaded green band is Ramadan 2027 (expected). The yellow area is daylight.

Times in Japan Standard Time. Japan does not use daylight saving.

Good to know

Ramadan 2027

About 8 Feb to 9 Mar 2027Expected dates. The start depends on moon sighting, so it can move by a day or two. Eid al-Fitr is expected around 9 to 10 March.
Eid al-Adha: about 16 to 17 May 2027Also expected, subject to moon sighting.
Why 2027 is easyRamadan moves about 11 days earlier each year. In 2027 it falls in winter, so fasting days in Tohoku are shorter than in summer.

Dates: Wego, Khaleej Times. Confirm with your local mosque.

The six times

FajrDawn prayer. Starts before sunrise.
SunriseNot a prayer. Fajr ends here.
DhuhrMidday prayer, after the sun passes its highest point.
AsrAfternoon prayer.
MaghribSunset prayer. Also the time to break fast in Ramadan.
IshaNight prayer, once twilight has gone.

Month by month

Times on the 15th of each month. ☾ marks the months that include Ramadan 2027.

How these are calculated. Times are computed from the sun's position, using the Muslim World League method (Fajr 18°, Isha 17°) and each city's coordinates. They are estimates, not an official timetable, and your mosque or app may differ by a few minutes. Always confirm locally. Cities: Sendai, Aomori, Akita, Morioka, Yamagata, Fukushima. Times in other Tohoku towns differ by a few minutes.
