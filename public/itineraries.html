Sample days

A day in Tohoku, planned around prayer

Pick a season and a place. See where the prayers fall, and how combining while travelling changes the day.

Please read. This planner shows timing only. It is not a ruling. Scholars differ on combining prayers while travelling (jam'). Many scholars of the Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali schools allow combining Dhuhr with Asr and Maghrib with Isha for a traveller under certain conditions, while the Hanafi school generally does not. The distance and conditions that count as travelling also vary. Ask your own imam or scholar what applies to you. Travel times here are estimates: check routes and timetables before you go. Prayer times are calculated estimates for Sendai, so confirm them with your mosque or app. Prayer spaces and halal food at each stop still need to be confirmed.
